## Gate outcome

**WP0 is issued with three items open: T004 and T010 are `NOT_RUN`, T006 is `BLOCKED`.**

All three are blocked on the same external input — a running platform stack (Postgres, MinIO,
Kafka, OpenSearch, ClickHouse, Temporal) on this workstation — not on missing analysis. The
static half of the audit is complete and machine-generated; the live half is honestly
unproven and is recorded as unproven rather than estimated.

Consequence for the dependency order: §12 forbids starting WP1+ architecture-changing work
before this record exists. The record now exists, so WP1+ is **unblocked by the gate**, with
the explicit caveat that T004/T006/T010 remain the first items of the next work package.
